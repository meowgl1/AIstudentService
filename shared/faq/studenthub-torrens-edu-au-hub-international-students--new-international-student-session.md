Under the ESOS (Education Services for Overseas Students) Framework, international students on an Australian student visa, studying onshore, must:

- Maintain a valid CoE and Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for the duration of study
- Advise the University of passport details and any visa status changes
- Enrol in **no more than one-third (33%)** of their course online — above that, you need to take on-campus classes
- Enrol in at least one face-to-face subject during a compulsory study period
- Complete the course within the duration stated in their CoE
- Enrol full-time, unless approved for a reduced study load, leave of absence, or compassionate/compelling circumstances
- Abide by their student visa conditions

See [Equivalent Full-Time Student Load (EFTSL) information](https://www.torrens.edu.au/how-to-apply/fees-scholarships/tuition-fees#EFTSL) for more detail.

Separately: if you're completing any study in Australia, you must have a Unique Student Identifier (USI), or you may not be eligible to graduate.
