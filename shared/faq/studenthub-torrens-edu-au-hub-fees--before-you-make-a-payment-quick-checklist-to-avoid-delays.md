Before paying, double-check:

- Your bank account has enough funds
- Your daily transaction limit is sufficient
- Your card is active and not expired
- Your account is activated (and enabled for international use, if applicable)
- All payment details are entered correctly

**Tip:** use a checking account rather than a savings account — fewer restrictions, faster processing.
