- **Credible sources** — must provide factual, unbiased information written by experts in the field. [Learn more about credible sources](http://library.torrens.edu.au/howtovideos/credibleinformation).
- **Peer-reviewed articles** — often expected for assessments; considered the gold standard of academic publishing. [Learn more about peer review](http://library.torrens.edu.au/howtovideos/peerreview).
