Use AND, OR, and NOT to connect search terms and broaden or narrow results:

- **AND** — each result contains **all** search terms
- **OR** — each result contains **at least one** search term
- **NOT** — results **do not contain** the specified terms

**Example:** (Ethics OR social implications OR controversy OR unethical OR intellectual property) AND (generative AI OR artificial intelligence OR AI art OR computer generated art)
