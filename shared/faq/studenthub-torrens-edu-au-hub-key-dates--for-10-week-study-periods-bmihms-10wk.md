BM Terms are compulsory ten-week study periods.

| Key date | Timing | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| International financial liability deadline | 1 day before the study period starts | International students only |
| Start date | Week 1 of the term | All students |
| Enrolment change deadline | Sunday of Week 2 | All students |
| Census date | Wednesday of Week 3 | Domestic students only |
| Academic withdrawal date | Friday of Week 5 | All students |
| Withdrawal deadline | Monday of Week 9 | All students |
| Finish date | End of the term | All students |
| Results release date | 1.5 weeks after the term ends | All students |
