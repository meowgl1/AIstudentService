If you need 1-6 weeks extra, you can be approved straight away with no reason or supporting evidence required. [Email student.finance@tua.edu.au](mailto:student.finance@tua.edu.au?subject=I%20would%20like%20a%20payment%20extension) to request it.

Also see the [Thrifty tips and money-saving guide](https://studenthub.torrens.edu.au/ld.php?content_id=53019756) for managing your budget.
