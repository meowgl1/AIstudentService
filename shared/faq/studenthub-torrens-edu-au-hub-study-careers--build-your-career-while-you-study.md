**WIL (Work Integrated Learning)** is industry-connected learning as part of your studies — depending on your course, it may include placements, internships, student clinic, live briefs, virtual simulations, or client projects.

**Careers Connect** is the digital career services portal, available on-demand, where you can:

- Get help with your CV and cover letters
- Access pre-approved WIL/placement/virtual internship opportunities
- Search and apply for casual, part-time and graduate jobs
- Practice with virtual/mock interviews
- Access job-search and career-planning events, resources and webinars
