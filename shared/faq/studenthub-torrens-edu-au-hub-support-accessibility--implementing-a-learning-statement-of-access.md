Approved students are issued a Learning Statement of Access, stating the formalised, pre-approved adjustments granted.

You're responsible for sharing it with academic/teaching staff at the start of every trimester (or as early as possible) so they understand what support to implement.

- Need a copy? [Contact Accessibility Services](mailto:accessibilityservices@torrens.edu.au)
- Specialised equipment/software adjustments are arranged during the application process
- Academic adjustments require timely communication with Academic/Campus staff, facilitated by the Accessibility Advisor
