1. Go to the [Student Portal](https://studentssb-prod.sis.torrens.edu.au/StudentSelfService), select "Student Access" and log in
2. Select "Subject Selection", then "Register for Classes"
3. Select the relevant term and your course, then click "Continue"
4. Select the next subject(s) per your course structure
5. Add the subject(s) you want
6. Click "Submit" to finalise your enrolment
