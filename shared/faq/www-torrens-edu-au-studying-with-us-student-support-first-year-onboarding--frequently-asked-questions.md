- Once your enrolment is finalised, the Onboarding team is your main contact — choose 1:1 or group sessions via phone, Microsoft Teams, or in person
- You'll get access to the Student Portal to select subjects; they can guide you based on your study mode, attendance requirements and study path (see your [course guide](https://studenthub.torrens.edu.au/Hub/Courses) for required subjects)
- You'll log into all university platforms with your student email address and password — they help you activate it
- They can connect you with Student Support, Success Coaches, IT Support, Counselling, Library and Learning Support, and other resources
- You'll get a [getting started](https://studenthub.torrens.edu.au/Hub/getting-started) pack with 5 steps to begin your study journey
