Use your assessment question to identify the key topics — they'll drive the focus of your background reading and search.

**Example question:** Identify and discuss two ethical issues around AI-generated art.
**Key topics:** ethical issues, AI-generated art

[Watch the Academic Success videos](https://library.torrens.edu.au/academicskills_as/assessment_briefs) to learn more about what your assessment question and brief are asking of you.
