Trimesters are 12 consecutive weeks. There are three compulsory trimesters per year: Trimester 1, Trimester 2, and Trimester 3.

| Key date | Timing | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| International financial liability deadline | 1 day before the study period starts | International students only |
| Start date | Week 1 of the study period | All students |
| Enrolment change deadline | Sunday of Week 2 | All students |
| Census date | Friday of Week 3 | Domestic students only |
| Academic withdrawal date | Friday of Week 7 | All students |
| Withdrawal deadline | Friday of Week 10 | All students |
| Finish date | End of the study period | All students |
| Results release date | 2 weeks after the study period ends | All students |
