Final results are released per subject at the end of the study period, generally two weeks after it finishes (check [Key Dates](https://studenthub.torrens.edu.au/Hub/Key-Dates) for exact timing). Release depends on final assessment/log book/hours submission, including where an extension was granted.

- Individual assessment results: published on your Online Learning Platform (except final assessments/exams)
- Final results: access via your [Student Portal](https://studenthub.torrens.edu.au/Hub/student-portal)
