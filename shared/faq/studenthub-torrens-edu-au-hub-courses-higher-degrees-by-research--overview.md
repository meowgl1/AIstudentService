Higher Degrees by Research (HDR) are offered at the [Adelaide](https://studenthub.torrens.edu.au/Hub/Campuses/Adelaide), [Brisbane](https://studenthub.torrens.edu.au/Hub/Campuses/Brisbane), [Melbourne](https://studenthub.torrens.edu.au/Hub/Campuses/Melbourne), and [Sydney](https://studenthub.torrens.edu.au/Hub/Campuses/Sydney) campuses, and [Online](https://studenthub.torrens.edu.au/Hub/Campuses/Online). A research degree deepens your understanding, expands your qualifications, and adds to your specialisation's body of knowledge.
