- **Deadline extension:** notify your lecturer no later than 3 days before the assessment deadline
- **Assessment format modification:** needs advice from Program Directors; notify lecturers at the start of each trimester
- **Exams:** notify Student Services and teaching staff of approved adjustments 2 weeks before exam week
- **Plan review:** your treating health practitioner recommends the adjustment duration; you can request a review anytime. Other Statements are reviewed at least every 2 years, and must be reviewed if you change course, campus, or study mode
