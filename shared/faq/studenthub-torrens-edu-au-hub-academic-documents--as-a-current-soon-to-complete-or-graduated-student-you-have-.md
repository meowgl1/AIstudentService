Available documents depend on your enrolment type:

- **Higher Education:** Official Academic Transcript*, Completion Letter, Testamur**
- **Vocational Education & Training:** Official Statement of Attainment*, Completion Letter, Testamur**

\* Only available once grades are published after your first study period.
\*\* Only released once you've provided your USI and all fees are cleared.

**How to request:**
1. Go to the [Academic Document Request Portal](https://academicdocumentrequest.torrens.edu.au/) (single sign-on, or enter your details directly)
2. Select the document type and quantity
3. Choose your delivery method
4. Confirm and pay — **fees are non-refundable**, so check your details before submitting

**Processing times:**
- Digital copy: usually issued to My eQuals within a week (minimum 10 working days if the document predates 2021, or you're a former student)
- Hard copy: minimum 10 working days for processing and postage
- Documents from before 2016: minimum 6-8 weeks
- Replacement testamur (lost/stolen/damaged): you may need to provide a [statutory declaration](https://www.ag.gov.au/legal-system/statutory-declarations)

Only finalised grades appear on a transcript — if you order before results are released, check [Key Dates](https://studenthub.torrens.edu.au/Hub/Key-Dates) and submit a new request afterwards.
