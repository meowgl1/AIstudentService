The SRC works to make student community better, through three pillars:

- **Community** — creating a welcoming, diverse, inclusive campus; running events and workshops for students to network and connect
- **Advocacy** — supporting students facing difficulty, referring them to the relevant campus team member, and handling feedback with empathy
- **Decision Making** — attending campus management meetings each trimester and being consulted on decisions that affect the student experience
