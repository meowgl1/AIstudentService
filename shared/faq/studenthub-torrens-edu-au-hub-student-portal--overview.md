The Student Portal is where you select subjects, register for classes, view your timetable, check grades, and view invoices.

[Log in to the Student Portal](https://studentssb-prod.sis.torrens.edu.au/StudentSelfService) with your Office 365 username and password.

Subject selection — when you pick subjects/classes for the next study period — has its own [Subject Selection page](https://studenthub.torrens.edu.au/Hub/subject-selection).
