Missing important dates, deadlines, and information due to not reading your student email will **not** be excused — no waivers or consideration are given.

Initial login information is sent to the personal email address on your student record (check junk/spam). If you never received it, [contact Student Services](https://studenthub.torrens.edu.au/Hub/Contact-Us).

- **New student:** can't find your activation email or having setup issues? Contact Student Services.
- **Current student:** for password resets, see the password-reset guide; for other issues, contact Student Services.
