MyLearn is the online learning platform, integrated with Microsoft Teams for classes, chat, and collaboration. With Teams you can:

- Log onto your online classes
- Easily share files with others
- Chat, connect and collaborate with Learning Facilitators and classmates

MyLearn also offers:

- Assignment feedback via video
- A mobile-friendly, fully accessible platform
- Assignment due-date calendars
- Collaboration via OneNote
- Searchable transcripts of recorded online classes
- Additional tools: Adobe Creative Suite, Clipchamp (MS Stream), Microsoft Office apps
