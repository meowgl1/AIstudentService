You're eligible for FEE-HELP for a unit of study if you:

- Are an Australian citizen studying at least part of your course in Australia; or are a New Zealand SCV holder or permanent humanitarian visa holder meeting residency requirements (permanent residents: approved bridging studies only)
- Are enrolled in a fee-paying place at a FEE-HELP provider
- Are enrolled in an eligible course by the census date
- Submit the Request for FEE-HELP loan form to your provider by the census date
- Have an available HELP balance
- Have a Unique Student Identifier (USI) before your first census date (for enrolments from 1 January 2021)

More detail: [Study Assist — FEE-HELP](https://www.studyassist.gov.au/help-loans/fee-help)
