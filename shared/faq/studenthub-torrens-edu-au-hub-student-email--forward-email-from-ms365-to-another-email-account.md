1. Sign in at [portal.office.com](https://portal.office.com/)
2. At the top of the page, choose Settings > Mail
3. Choose Forwarding (if you don't see this option, it's not available for your account)
4. You can forward email to one other account

Full instructions are also on the [Microsoft website](https://support.office.com/en-us/article/forward-email-from-office-365-to-another-email-account-1ed4ee1e-74f8-4f53-a174-86b748ff6a0e).
