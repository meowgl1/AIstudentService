**Modified arrangements** (deadline extension, exam deferral, re-submission/re-assessment/resit, result adjustment, or a second WIL placement attempt): submit a completed Application for Assessment Special Consideration.

**Unfair/inappropriate marking:** you can request a re-assessment — your work is graded again by a different assessor. For when/how to apply, see the Assessment Policy:
- Higher Education courses: "Requesting a re-mark" section
- Vocational Education courses: "Requesting a re-assessment" section
