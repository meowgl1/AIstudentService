A space to connect with First Nations students and allies, share stories and culture, learn about First Nations perspectives, and get practical support for university life.

Open to all students — you don't need to identify as Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander to participate. Members are asked to listen respectfully and protect the privacy and culture shared by others in the group.
