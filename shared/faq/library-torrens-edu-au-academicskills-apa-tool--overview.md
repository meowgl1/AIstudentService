  - "Do I need to cite every time I use someone else's idea?"
  - "What's the difference between parenthetical and narrative citations?"
---
- Whenever you use someone else's ideas or information to support your assessment, add an in-text citation.
- Each in-text citation must correspond to an entry in your reference list.
- You can cite the same source more than once if needed.
- In-text citations are either **parenthetical** or **narrative**.
