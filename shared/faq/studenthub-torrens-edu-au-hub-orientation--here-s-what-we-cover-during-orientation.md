- **Understanding university life** — academic expectations and what it takes to succeed
- **Navigating your course** — course structure, learning outcomes, assessment/industry/employability requirements
- **Accessing essential systems** — logging into the online learning platform and student email
- **Exploring services** — support services, campus environment, safety, local amenities
- **Embracing inclusivity** — campus culture around diversity, equity and inclusion
- **Setting career goals** — how your degree connects to your career aspirations
