Academic sources are typically written by experts in the field, such as academics and researchers:

- **Scholarly books & eBooks** — good for general, established information across a broad range of topics
- **Scholarly journal articles** — the most up-to-date information and research, often [peer-reviewed](http://library.torrens.edu.au/howtovideos/peerreview)
