The Academic Progression Committee assesses your progress based on final results each study period. You're making satisfactory progress if you:

- Pass/meet requirements for at least 50% of enrolled credit points in a study period
- Complete your course within the maximum study period (or by your CoE end date)
- Meet any additional course-specific progression requirements

See the Academic Progression Policy for full detail.

Your GPA is a numerical calculation based on the credit points and grade points achieved over a study period or course.
