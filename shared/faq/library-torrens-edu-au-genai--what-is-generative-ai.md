Generative AI (GenAI) creates new content by identifying and predicting patterns in existing data, in response to a user prompt — unlike AI systems that just analyse or classify information.

GenAI can generate:

- Text
- Images
- Audio and music
- Video
- Computer code

Example uses: brainstorming/summarising/writing help, generating images from descriptions, creating audio/music/video, and assisting with coding.
