Before requesting, note that extensions can delay feedback, future assessments, and sometimes progression to later subjects.

- **1 to 7 calendar days** — no supporting documentation needed, but you must give a detailed explanation. Submit via the "Assignments" section on MyLearn, at least 3 calendar days before the due date (unless the delay was unavoidable).
- **8 to 14 calendar days** — documentation required. Same MyLearn submission process and 3-day notice.
- **Over 14 calendar days** — use the Assessment Special Consideration Form ([Torrens University](https://torrens.blackboard.com/bbcswebdav/xid-4652143_1) | [Think Education](https://torrens.blackboard.com/bbcswebdav/xid-2796802_1)) and submit it with supporting documents to your Learning Facilitator. This form also covers WIL placement deferral and other modified assessment arrangements.

If you're not on MyLearn, submit your request directly to your Learning Facilitator(s).

If you need extra support, [Study Support](https://studenthub.torrens.edu.au/Hub/Study-Support) and the [Counselling service](https://studenthub.torrens.edu.au/Hub/Support/Counselling) are available.
