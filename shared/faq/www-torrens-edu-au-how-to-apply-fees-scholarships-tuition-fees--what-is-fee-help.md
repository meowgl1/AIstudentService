FEE-HELP is a loan scheme helping eligible domestic fee-paying students pay tuition fees. Only available to domestic students; may exclude permanent residency visa holders.

- **Loan limit (2026 census dates):** $129,883 for most students; $186,544 for approved medicine, dentistry, veterinary science, and certain aviation courses. Indexation doesn't count toward your limit.
- **Loan fee:** a 20% FEE-HELP loan fee applies to some undergraduate study, but **not** if your census date is on/after 1 January 2019 and you're at a Table B provider like Torrens.
- **Repayment:** starts via the tax system once you earn above the compulsory repayment threshold ($67,000 for the 2025-2026 income year).
