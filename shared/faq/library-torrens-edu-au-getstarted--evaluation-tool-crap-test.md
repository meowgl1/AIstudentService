The CRAP test checks a source's credibility across four areas:

- **Currency** — How recent is it? When was it published/last updated? If older, is it based on established knowledge?
- **Reliability** — Does it seem balanced, and is it backed by research, evidence, and references? Is it [peer-reviewed](https://library.torrens.edu.au/howtovideos/peerreview)?
- **Authority** — Are the author and publisher qualified and expert in the subject area?
- **Purpose/point of view** — What's the intent? Is there bias? Is it relevant to your purpose?

If you're unsure about a source, ask your Learning Facilitator or [contact the library via Chat](http://faq.torrens.edu.au/chat/widget/9191db96e76bee9bc5f41bb9b313bfff).
