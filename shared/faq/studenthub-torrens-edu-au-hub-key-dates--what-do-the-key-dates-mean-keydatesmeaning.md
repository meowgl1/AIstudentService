- **International financial liability deadline** — deadline for an international student to finalise enrolment before financial liability is incurred. Subject fees are due per your invoice date. You must study full time.
- **Enrolment change deadline** — last day to add/change subjects or class times via the online subject selection tool (end of Week 2 for trimesters).
- **Census date** — domestic-student deadline to withdraw from a subject without financial or academic penalty (end of Week 3 for trimesters; Week 2 for shorter periods). Withdraw by this date: no tuition fee, no transcript entry. Withdraw after: no refund.
- **Academic withdrawal date** — deadline to withdraw without academic penalty. You still pay the fees, but get "Withdrawn Not Fail" instead of a Fail grade.
- **Withdrawal deadline** — last day to withdraw at all. You still pay the fees, and your transcript shows "Withdrawn Fail". Withdrawing after this date shows as "Fail".
- **Results release date** — when the Examination Committee finalises and publishes your results in the Student Portal, usually 2 weeks after the study period ends.
