AI refers to computer systems that perform tasks normally involving human judgement, pattern recognition, or language understanding — by analysing data to find patterns, not through true understanding. That means AI output can sometimes be inaccurate, biased, incomplete, or misleading.

Everyday examples already embedded in digital tools:

- Your phone suggesting the next word while typing
- Search engines predicting what you're looking for
- Study tools generating summaries
- Research databases recommending related articles
- Writing tools suggesting grammar or clarity improvements

At university, AI tools should support your learning and thinking, not replace them — human judgement and critical review remain essential.
