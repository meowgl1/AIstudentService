- Always use your official student email address when contacting the university — staff can only accept/respond to official student addresses, to verify identity and protect privacy
- Set your personal account to always reply using your connected student email address
- Forwarding university email to a non-university account is at your own risk — the university doesn't manage or guarantee delivery of forwarded mail
- Check your [student inbox via the web](https://outlook.office.com/mail/) regularly to confirm mail is arriving/forwarding and you haven't hit your storage limit
