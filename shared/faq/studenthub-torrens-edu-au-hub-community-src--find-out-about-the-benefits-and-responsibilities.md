**Benefits:**
- Develop leadership skills
- Make lasting friendships and impact your community
- Learn teamwork and organisational skills
- Access exclusive development workshops for SRC members
- Be formally recognised for your contribution

**Responsibilities:**
- Bringing community events to life
- Hosting fundraising initiatives
- Helping the campus make better decisions for students
- Advocating for and representing the student body

For campus-specific SRC contacts and members, check your campus's SRC page.
