Academic Success helps you figure out which study approaches work best for you, through online resources, workshops, and the PASS (Peer Assisted Study Sessions) Program. It can help with:

- Understanding your assessment brief
- Critical reading and thinking skills
- Research and writing skills
- APA 7th edition referencing and academic integrity

See also [Class4Success](https://mylearn.torrens.edu.au/courses/5553) in MyLearn.
