My eQuals is the platform Torrens/Think use to digitally issue secure, verified academic documents to students.

To share a document with a third party (another institution, an employer, etc.):
1. Request a digital copy of the document via the [Academic Document Request Portal](https://academicdocumentrequest.torrens.edu.au/)
2. Once it's uploaded to My eQuals, share it directly with whomever you need, for as long as you need

See the [My eQuals learner FAQ](https://www.myequals.edu.au/learner-faqs) for more detail.
